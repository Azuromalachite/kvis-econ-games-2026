KVIS Econ Games 2026

Playful economics: browser games built for the classroom.

Three games, numbered by the week they belong to. Each one runs in the browser, with nothing to install and no account to make. Pick a week and press Play.

Week 1 · Han–Roman era

Silk Road Expedition Simulator

Lead a caravan from Chang’an to Rome: exchange currencies, trade cargo, hire guards and guides, and manage water along the route.

trade · logistics · exchange

Play Silk Road Expedition Simulator

Week 2 · c. 1500–1600 CE

Frost & Silver

Run an Andean ayllu of about 1,000 people through six harvests, first under Inca rules and then under the colonial mita that sent men to the silver mines of Potosí. Estimates have output per person rising a little across the 1500s. Did life get better?

strategy · climate · colonial economy

Play Frost & Silver Read the walkthrough for Frost & Silver

Week 3 · c. 1000–1450 CE

Monsoon Navigator

Plan an Indian Ocean trading voyage around the monsoon calendar. Ride the seasonal winds, dodge cyclones and pirates, manage hull, debt and tariffs, and turn a profit over a 24-month venture.

trade · Indian Ocean · risk management

Play Monsoon Navigator
