It uses the game's own model, read from projects/02/index.html and never changed, with the 18 November sweep's ten ranges, 500 Latin-hypercube draws (same generator seed) and weather seeds 1–40, crossed with two switches:

  • S1, the player: the default plan versus the plan chosen in advance (ADVANCE_PLAN), applied before every harvest.
  • S2, the tribute: a fixed total set at the 1573 count (the game) versus re-counted each colonial harvest.

"The lesson holds" for a draw: averaged over its 40 seeds, GDP per person rises at least 3 index points and food per person falls at least 50 kcal a day, Inca harvests to colonial harvests. Results are deterministic.
