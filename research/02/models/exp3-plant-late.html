Experiment 3: does planting late give the Pleiades forecast decision value?

Pre-registration: research/pre-registrations/2026-11-19-plant-late.md (merged in #46 before this page existed). Serve the repository root (python -m http.server) and open /research/02/models/exp3-plant-late.html. It uses the game's own model, read from frost-and-silver.html and never changed, with one textual replacement in grow: a late planting loses dL in a dry year instead of 25% and costs c in a normal one. No new random draw, so every seed's weather is the game's.

  • Policies: never late (the game today), always late, late when the Pleiades rose hazy, late in dry years only (perfect knowledge: a ceiling nobody can choose).
  • Grid: dL ∈ {5, 10, 15, 20}%, c ∈ {2, 3, 5, 8, 10, 12}%. Holdout seeds 81–400; seeds 1–80 replicate the pilot.
  • Player: ADVANCE_PLAN before every harvest; the default plan (best land first at each re-plan) at 10% / 5%.
