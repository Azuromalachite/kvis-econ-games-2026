Atlas market check

Checks the Atlas trade panel and price chart against the game's own engine functions. Serve the repository root over HTTP (for example python3 -m http.server) and open /tests/03/atlas-market-check.html. ?page=<same-origin path> checks another copy of the page (used for planted defects).

Ground truth is computed here, independently of the adapter: sell price = getLivePortPrice; buy price = Math.round(sell × (1 + getEffectiveTariff(port))), as in buyCargoQty; and the labels are also checked against the cash moved by one real buyCargoQty and sellCargoQty.
